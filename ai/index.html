FAQ

Common questions.

How realistic are Mada AI interviews?
You speak out loud with an AI interviewer who states the case, answers your questions with the case facts, pushes on your logic and asks you to summarise, much like a first-round interview. It is practice, not a replica of any firm’s process.
Can I choose my AI interviewer?
That is the plan: six interviewers with different seniority and style. Tatiana, the partner-style interviewer, opens first and the others follow one at a time.
What is the difference between case and fit interviews?
A case interview tests how you structure and solve a business problem, with numbers. A fit interview tests your motivation, your stories and how you work with others. Case practice opens first; fit practice follows.
What feedback will I receive?
A structured report: a score for each skill, backed by quotes from your own interview, with strengths and the specific things to improve. It is a Mada developmental assessment, not official employer scoring.
Can I practice multiple times?
Yes. Each interview is a separate session with its own report, and we will vary the case so you do not repeat one.
What is included in each pricing plan?
The plans above show what we intend to offer. Pricing and inclusions are confirmed when each plan opens, and we will email the waitlist first.
How does Mada AI complement human coaching?
Use Mada AI to practise as often as you like. Use a Mada coach for personal feedback, strategy and high-stakes decisions from someone who has interviewed candidates at consulting firms.
