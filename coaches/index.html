How we choose coaches

Who coaches at Mada.

Where they’ve worked

Coaches come from MBB, Tier 2 strategy firms such as Strategy&, Oliver Wyman, Kearney, Roland Berger and Arthur D. Little, and Big Four strategy practices. Some are current consultants coaching alongside their role.

How they’re vetted

Every coach is interviewed personally by Hani before joining. We look for people who have interviewed or hired, and who can give direct, specific feedback.

How you’re matched

Hani matches every candidate to a coach based on target firms, office, level and the step you’re at. If you have a preference, tell us on the intro call.
