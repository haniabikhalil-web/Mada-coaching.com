Questions

Before you book.

Are you a recruitment agency?
No. We don’t place candidates into jobs and we have no relationship with any employer. We make you much more effective at navigating consulting recruiting yourself.
Who will coach me?
One of our experienced coaches, every one vetted by the Mada team. We match each candidate to a coach based on your target firms, your level and what you need. If you have a preference, say so on the intro call.
What happens on the free intro call?
Twenty minutes, no charge and no obligation. You tell us where you are and what you’re targeting. We tell you where to start, or tell you honestly if we’re not the right help.
What’s the difference between Mada coaching and Mada AI?
Coaching is 1:1 with an experienced consultant or interviewer, for personal guidance and high-stakes decisions. Mada AI is a set of self-serve tools for practice and repetition, coming soon. They work well together.
Do you guarantee an offer?
No, and you should be cautious of anyone who does. We have no role in any employer’s hiring decision. What we improve is how well prepared and positioned you are when it’s made.
